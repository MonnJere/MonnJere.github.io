## About Me

Hi, I’m a senior computer science student who likes building apps that fix real-world problems. I first got curious about tech because I wanted to know what really happens behind the scenes. That curiosity pushed me to dig into both the front-end interfaces people see and the back-end systems that keep everything running.

I grew up with gadgets all around me, so it was easy to fall in love with how digital tools can change daily life. Eventually I moved from just using those tools to making them. I get the same buzz from sketching a clean mobile layout as I do from writing a fast database query that no one ever notices.

Game design has grabbed my attention lately. I’ve put together a few small games that mix fun mechanics with simple stories. Those projects taught me how to keep players engaged, give clear feedback, and balance the line between challenge and reward.

When I start a new project I look at the whole picture. Good code matters, but so does design and the way people feel while using the product. I carry that idea into side projects where I try out new frameworks or just test odd ideas for fun.

Outside of code I stay balanced with a bunch of hobbies. Rock climbing lets me tackle problems with my hands and feet. Cooking feels like running experiments you can eat. Reading pulls in fresh ideas, and gaming shows me clever ways to handle interaction and pacing.

All those activities loop back into my work. Each one gives me a different angle that helps me write better software and come up with fresh ideas.
